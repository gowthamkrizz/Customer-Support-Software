Frequently Asked Service Questions

Everything you need to know about our dedicated call center deployment, VoIP infrastructure, and enterprise SLAs.

How quickly can Stackly deploy a dedicated support team?

Our standard deployment timeline is 7 to 14 business days for shared or dedicated agent teams of up to 50 reps. This covers SIP trunk routing configuration, CRM two-way integration, dynamic call script building, and rigorous agent product evaluation drills.

Rapid 48-hour emergency surge deployment available upon request.
Can you integrate directly with our existing CRM (Salesforce, Zendesk, HubSpot)?

Yes. We support native two-way API integrations with Salesforce, Zendesk, HubSpot, Freshdesk, Zoho, Shopify, Jira, and custom REST API endpoints. Customer interaction logs, call recordings, audio transcripts, and sentiment scores synchronize in sub-second real time.

Two-way contact and ticket status sync with zero data loss.
What security and compliance certifications do your call centers adhere to?

Our call centers operate in strict adherence with PCI-DSS Level 1 (with DTMF audio masking so agents never hear credit card digits), HIPAA for medical patient privacy, SOC 2 Type II certified cloud facilities, and GDPR/CCPA frameworks.

AES-256 bit audio encryption in transit and at rest.
How do you handle sudden call spikes during peak holiday seasons?

We maintain an elastic surge pool of cross-trained reserve agents and conversational AI voice bots capable of absorbing 300%+ inbound volume surges without degrading average speed of answer (ASA) or increasing queue hold times.

On-demand burst capacity activated with 4-hour notice.
Do you provide bilingual and multilingual customer support?

Yes. Beyond native English, our global contact delivery centers provide dedicated teams in Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Mandarin, Japanese, Arabic, and 24 other languages with native cultural nuance.

30+ spoken and written languages supported across voice and chat.
